TAT (Turn-Around Time) Analysis Report
Fulfilment Time Analysis · Closed AOG Demands

Turnaround time (TAT) measures the number of days from demand creation to closure. Out of 705 total events, 671 have been closed. The average TAT is 37 days with a median of 31 days, indicating a right-skewed distribution driven by a small number of long-running demands (max 181 days). Reducing TAT on P1 demands is critical to fleet readiness.

Key Observations
  • 55% of demands are resolved within 30 days — reflects efficient procurement for standard parts.
  • 6% of demands exceed 90 days, likely involving OEM-sourced or single-vendor critical components.
  • SU-30 MKI drives the highest absolute number of closed events (319 total) and therefore the most TAT data.
  • Reducing the 90+ day tail would require strategic stock buffering of slow-moving critical spares.
  • P1 priority demands that exceed 45 days represent direct impact on mission readiness and should be escalated.
